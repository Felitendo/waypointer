# Changelog

## v0.1.0

_2026-11-15_

Welcome to Waypointer `v0.1.0`, the first release! It brings TinyHand's toy hand on a stick to
Linux: point at things, tap and draw on your screen while you share it, record it or present.

### Highlights

- A toy hand on a stick that follows your cursor: 36 hands, a laser dot, or one you make yourself
- Drag to draw smooth lines with a faint glow; hold **Ctrl** for an arrowhead
- A toolbar at the bottom: rest on the ink button for the colours, on the speaker for the volume
- Let clicks through to your apps with the ink button or a double tap on **Shift**, the hand stays
- Drag pointers into the toolbar, sort them, and pick a colour for each one
- Shortcuts, a tray icon and the `waypointer` command to drive it all
